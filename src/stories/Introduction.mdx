# 🏥 DrCloud EHR Design System

**A comprehensive, healthcare-first design system built for modern Electronic Health Record applications.**

Welcome to the DrCloud Design System - your complete UI component library specifically designed for healthcare applications. Our atomic design methodology ensures consistency, accessibility, and safety across all patient-facing and clinical interfaces.

## 🎯 Healthcare-First Design Philosophy

Our design system is built with healthcare professionals and patients in mind:

- **Patient Safety**: Clear visual hierarchy and error prevention patterns
- **Accessibility**: WCAG 2.1 AA compliance for all users
- **Clinical Workflow**: Components designed for medical terminology and workflows
- **HIPAA Compliance**: Privacy and security considerations built into every component
- **Touch-Friendly**: Optimized for mobile and tablet use in clinical settings

## 🧬 Atomic Design Structure

Our components are organized using Brad Frost's Atomic Design methodology:

### **⚛️ Atoms**
The foundational building blocks of our healthcare UI:
- **Button** - Actions and CTAs for clinical workflows
- **Input** - Form fields for patient data entry
- **Label** - Accessible form labeling
- **Badge** - Status indicators and alerts
- **Avatar** - Healthcare professional and patient representation
- **Checkbox** - Consent forms and medical history
- **Spinner** - Loading states for medical data
- **Select** - Dropdown selections for clinical data

### **🔬 Molecules**
Functional groups of atoms that work together:
- **NotificationCard** - Patient alerts and system notifications
- **Form Groups** - Complete input with validation
- **Navigation** - Clinical workflow navigation

### **🧪 Organisms**
Complex components for complete sections:
- **Patient Dashboard** - Complete patient overview
- **Medical Charts** - Clinical data visualization
- **Appointment Scheduler** - Booking and management

### **📋 Templates**
Page-level layouts for clinical workflows:
- **Patient Record Templates**
- **Dashboard Layouts**
- **Clinical Form Templates**

## 🎨 Design Tokens

Our design system uses a comprehensive token system for:

- **Colors**: Medical-safe color palette with accessibility in mind
- **Typography**: Clear, readable fonts for clinical environments
- **Spacing**: Consistent spacing for touch targets (44px minimum)
- **Shadows**: Subtle depth for card-based medical interfaces
