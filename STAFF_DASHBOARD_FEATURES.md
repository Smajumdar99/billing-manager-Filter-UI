# Staff Dashboard Features Guide

## What is the Staff Dashboard?
The Staff Dashboard helps behavioral health counselors manage their patients. It shows all patients where the counselor is either the main provider or part of the care team.

## Main Features

### 1. Metrics Section
**What it shows:**
- **Total**: How many patients you have overall
- **Active Since 2024**: Patients who are currently active 
- **Discharged This Month**: Patients who were discharged recently
- **High Risk**: Patients who need immediate attention

**How it works:**
- On desktop: Always visible at the top in one row
- On mobile: Can be hidden/shown by tapping the metrics button
- Click any metric to filter the patient list

### 2. Search Feature
**What it does:**
- Find patients by typing their name
- Search works instantly as you type
- Searches through patient names and IDs

**Where to find it:**
- Big search box at the top
- Has a magnifying glass icon
- Type anything to start searching

### 3. Category Tabs
**What they are:**
- **Admitted**: Shows active patients
- **Discharged**: Shows patients who left the program

**How to use:**
- Click a tab to see only those patients
- Active tab is highlighted in blue

### 4. Filters
**What you can filter by:**
- **Status**: Active, Discharged, etc.
- **Clinician**: Which staff member
- **Due Date**: When something is due
- **Facility**: Which location
- **Location**: Specific building/room

**How filters work:**
- On desktop: Always visible below the search
- On mobile: Hidden by default, tap "Filters" to show them
- Active filters show a number badge
- "Clear all filters" button resets everything

**Special filter:**
- "Only show incomplete" checkbox shows patients with missing treatment plans

### 5. Patient Cards (Mobile View)
**What each card shows:**
- Patient name and ID number
- Status badge (Active, Discharged)
- Program they're in
- Their provider/counselor
- Important dates (birth date, admitted date, last seen)
- Next appointment (highlighted if it's today)
- Treatment plan status with color-coded icons
- Action buttons (Chart, Plan, Schedule)

**How cards work:**
- Tap anywhere on a card to select the patient
- Cards are stacked vertically
- Easy to scroll through on phone

### 6. Data Table (Desktop View)
**What it shows:**
- All patient information in rows and columns
- Same information as cards but in table format
- Better for scanning lots of patients quickly

**Table features:**
- Sort by clicking column headers
- Resize columns by dragging
- Scroll horizontally if needed

### 7. Treatment Plan Status
**Color system:**
- **Red icon**: Not completed yet or overdue
- **Green icon**: Completed on time
- **Yellow icon**: In progress

**What it tracks:**
- **14-Day Plan**: Initial treatment plan (due 14 days after admission)
- **MDTP**: Master Discharge Treatment Plan

### 8. Action Buttons
**Chart button:** View patient's medical records
**Plan button:** See treatment plans
**Schedule button:** Book appointments

### 9. Responsive Design
**On phones/tablets:**
- Metrics can be hidden to save space
- Filters are collapsible 
- Patients shown as cards
- Touch-friendly buttons

**On computers:**
- Everything visible at once
- Metrics always shown at top
- Patients shown in table format
- More information fits on screen

### 10. Special Indicators
**Today's appointments:**
- Orange corner triangle on patient cards
- Orange text for appointment time
- "TODAY" badge clearly visible

**High-risk patients:**
- Red "High Risk" badge
- Shows in metrics count
- Easy to spot for priority attention

## How to Use the Dashboard

### Quick Patient Search:
1. Type patient name in search box
2. Results filter automatically
3. Click on patient to view details

### Filter by Status:
1. Click the status metric (like "Active")
2. Or use the status dropdown in filters
3. List updates to show only those patients

### Find Overdue Treatment Plans:
1. Check "Only show incomplete" box
2. Look for red icons next to patient names
3. Click patient to take action

### View Today's Appointments:
1. Look for orange corner triangles on cards
2. Or click "Today Appointments" if available
3. Patients with today's appointments show first

### Clear All Searches/Filters:
1. Click "Clear all filters" button
2. Or refresh the page
3. Dashboard returns to showing all patients

## Tips for Best Use

- **Start with metrics** to get overview of your caseload
- **Use search** when looking for specific patients
- **Check "High Risk" metric** first thing each day
- **Filter by "incomplete"** to see what needs attention
- **Mobile users**: Hide metrics after checking to see more patients
- **Desktop users**: Keep metrics visible for quick reference

This dashboard is designed to help you manage your patients efficiently while ensuring no one falls through the cracks.
